Mole Repeller Next Generation — Hardware Wiring
================================================

MCU: Arduino Pro Mini 5V / 16MHz (ATmega328P)

─── VIBRATION MOTOR CIRCUIT ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Arduino D9 ──[1kΩ]──► NPN base  (e.g. 2N2222 / BC547 / PN2222)
                          NPN emitter → GND
                          NPN collector ──► Motor (-)
                          Motor (+) ──► 5V
                          Flyback diode: cathode → 5V, anode → Motor (-)
                              (IN4001 or 1N4148 across motor terminals)

  Suitable motors: 3–5V DC vibration coin motor, 1027 or 1020 type
  PWM control → adjustable intensity (0–255)

─── SPEAKER CIRCUIT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Option A — Transistor (louder, recommended for outdoor use)
  Arduino D3 ──[1kΩ]──► NPN base
                          NPN emitter → GND
                          NPN collector ──► Speaker (-) 8Ω
                          Speaker (+) ──► 5V via 47Ω series resistor
                              (limits peak current to ~100mA)

  Option B — Direct drive (small indoor speaker, simpler)
  Arduino D3 ──[100Ω]──► Speaker (+)
                          Speaker (-) → GND
                          Max ~40mA, volume limited

  Recommended: 8Ω 0.5W–2W speaker, 36mm diameter or larger for better
  low-frequency response. Larger cone = more ground vibration coupling.

─── POWER ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Option A — 3× AA alkaline (4.5V) → VCC pin directly
             Estimated runtime: 100–300 h depending on soil type and sleep ratio

  Option B — 18650 LiPo (3.7V) + TP4056 charger → 5V boost module → VCC
             Rechargeable via micro-USB

  Use 3.3V / 8MHz Pro Mini to halve active power consumption.
  WDT deep sleep draws ~5µA; motor/speaker pulses are the dominant load.

─── ANALOG ENTROPY PIN ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  A0 — leave floating (not connected). Used only at startup to seed the PRNG
  by sampling ADC noise. Do NOT connect to anything.

─── STATUS LED ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  D13 — onboard LED (Pro Mini has one built in)
  Blinks 3× on power-up, then lights solid during each active cycle.

─── OPTIONAL ENCLOSURE NOTES ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Mount speaker face-down or couple it to a ground spike for max soil coupling
  - Vibration motor: glue to metal or wooden stake driven into ground
  - Weatherproof box: IP65 ABS with cable glands for motor/speaker leads
  - Spike length: 30–50 cm for best propagation through soil
