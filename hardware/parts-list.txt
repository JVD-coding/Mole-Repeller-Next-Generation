Mole Repeller Next Generation — Parts List
==========================================
Target MCU: ESP32 DevKit C

─── MICROCONTROLLER ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   ESP32 DevKit C                Dual-core 240MHz, 520KB RAM, built-in
                                     BLE + WiFi, deep sleep ~10µA, 3.3V GPIO
                                     Arduino IDE compatible (esp32 board package)

─── 12V VIBRATION MOTOR + RELAY ────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   12V DC vibration motor        775-series DC motor with bolt-on eccentric
                                     weight, or dedicated 12V vibration motor
                                     e.g. RS Pro / Mouser 12V vibration motor
                                     Much higher energy output than coin motors
   1   Relay module (1-channel)      5V coil, 10A/250VAC contacts
                                     Optocoupler input — use 3.3V-compatible
                                     module (e.g. KEYES with HEF4001) to connect
                                     directly to ESP32 GPIO, OR use Option B below
   1   Diode                         1N4001  (flyback across motor terminals)
   1   Capacitor                     100nF ceramic  (across motor terminals)

  Option B — level shift if relay module needs 5V signal:
   1   NPN transistor                2N2222A
   2   Resistor                      1kΩ  ¼W  (base + pull-up)

─── SPEAKER OUTPUT — OPTION A (simple NPN, bench testing) ──────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   Speaker                       8Ω  0.5W minimum
   1   NPN transistor                2N2222A  (or BC547)
   1   Resistor                      1kΩ  ¼W  (base resistor)
   1   Resistor                      47Ω  ¼W  (collector current limiter)

─── SPEAKER OUTPUT — OPTION B (LM386 amplifier, recommended for outdoor) ───────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   Speaker                       8Ω  1W–2W, 57mm diameter or larger
                                     Larger cone = better low-freq soil coupling
   1   IC                            LM386N-1  audio power amplifier  (DIP-8)
   1   Capacitor                     10µF  electrolytic  (gain boost, pins 1–8)
   1   Capacitor                     10µF  electrolytic  (bypass, pin 7)
   1   Capacitor                     250µF electrolytic  10V rating min
                                     (output coupling to speaker)
   1   Capacitor                     100µF electrolytic  (power decoupling)
   1   Resistor / potentiometer      1kΩ–10kΩ  (input limiting / volume)

─── POWER ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Option A — Dual supply (simplest split)
  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   USB power bank  (5V)          Powers ESP32 DevKit + relay coil
   8   AA alkaline battery           8× AA in series = ~12V for motor
   1   AA battery holder             8× AA with leads and fuse

  Option B — Single 12V supply (recommended for permanent install)
  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   12V Li-ion pack               3S 18650 pack (11.1V nominal, ~12.6V full)
                                     e.g. 3× 18650 in series holder
   1   BMS / protection board        3S BMS with overcharge + short protection
   1   Buck converter                XL4016 or LM2596 module, set to 5V output
                                     Powers ESP32 + relay coil from 12V rail
   1   Charger                       12.6V CC/CV charger for 3S pack

  Option C — 12V SLA (very long runtime, heavy)
  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   12V SLA battery               7Ah sealed lead-acid
   1   Voltage regulator             LM7805 or HT7550 → 5V for ESP32

─── PASSIVE COMPONENTS (general) ───────────────────────────────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   2   Capacitor                     100nF ceramic  (decoupling near ESP32 VIN
                                     and relay module 5V)

─── MECHANICAL / ENCLOSURE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   Enclosure                     IP65 ABS weatherproof box
                                     min. 120×80×50mm (ESP32 + relay + LM386)
   1   Ground stake                  Steel rod or rebar, 30–50cm, 10–16mm dia
                                     Larger diameter = better soil contact
   2   Cable gland                   PG7 or M12 for motor and speaker leads
   1   Speaker mount plate           Steel or aluminium flat plate
                                     Face-down on soil for ground coupling

─── OPTIONAL / FUTURE FEATURES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Qty  Part                          Spec / Notes
  ---  ----------------------------  -----------------------------------------
   1   Geophone                      SM-24 or GS-100 (mole detection)
                                     Connects to ESP32 ADC via LM358 pre-amp
   1   Op-amp                        LM358  (geophone signal pre-amplifier)

                                     One per unit in a multi-unit deployment

─── TOOLS / CONSUMABLES ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Soldering iron + solder
  - Heat shrink tubing (assorted)
  - Hook-up wire (22 AWG stranded for signal, 18 AWG for 12V motor leads)
  - Fuse holder + 2A fuse  (inline on 12V motor lead)
  - Epoxy or silicone sealant  (weatherproofing cable entries)
  - USB-A to Micro-USB cable  (programming ESP32 DevKit C)
  - Multimeter  (continuity, voltage checks)
